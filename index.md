
# Crazy Beat – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 26, 2026**

Crazy Beat is designed to bring useful health-related tools and everyday utilities together in one app. We respect your privacy and want you to understand what information may be used when you use the app.

This policy explains what information Crazy Beat may access, why certain permissions are needed, and how information may be handled by the services behind the app.

## 1. Information and Permissions

Depending on which features you choose to use, Crazy Beat may request access to:

- **Camera:** Used for camera-based heart-rate measurement and other camera features.
- **Microphone:** Used for voice-related features.
- **Location:** Used by features that need your device's location.
- **Health-related information:** Information you enter or readings produced by health-related tools, such as heart-rate measurements, may be processed to provide the relevant features.
- **Information you submit:** Text, URLs, or other content you choose to enter into AI, scanning, or other online features.

You do not need to use every feature. You can choose not to grant a permission, although the feature that needs it may not work.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the app is used to operate the feature you choose, provide results, improve the app's functionality, and respond to requests you submit.

Some features may need an internet connection and may send the information you provide to Crazy Beat's backend or the relevant third-party service to complete your request.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Certain features may rely on external services, such as AI providers or online processing services. When you use those features, the information needed to complete your request may be sent to the relevant service.

Those services handle information under their own privacy policies and terms. Crazy Beat does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party providers.

## 4. Storage and Security

Some app preferences or feature history may be stored on your device. Information sent to online services may also be processed by those services as needed to provide the feature.

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Sharing of Information

Crazy Beat does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate a feature you choose, or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Crazy Beat is not intended to collect personal information from children unnecessarily. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

## 7. Your Choices

You can manage app permissions through your Android device settings. You can also choose not to use features that require camera, microphone, location, or online processing.

## 8. Health Disclaimer

Crazy Beat's heart-rate and other health-related tools are intended for general informational purposes only. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Measurements may not always be accurate.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, with the updated date shown above.
